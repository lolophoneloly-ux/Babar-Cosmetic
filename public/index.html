OUR STORY

Started in a small studio. Built on one idea.

Babar Cosmetics was founded by Ahmad Hassan Babar, who started out mixing shades on a kitchen table and testing them on family before anyone else. The idea was simple: pigment that performs like the luxury brands, without the heavy, pore-clogging formulas that come with them.

Every product is still made in small batches, tested on real skin tones, and signed off personally before it ships. That's the part of the process we've refused to scale away.
